  3. OneClick Ads

    oneclickads.com.br

    Founder and technical lead

    A performance platform for Meta Ads. It tracks conversions by UTM, builds the dashboards and optimises campaigns on its own, around the clock: it scales the ad sets that sell, pauses the ones that do not and redistributes budget across every campaign in the account, through Meta's official API.

    Where Meta's automated rules use fixed thresholds, OneClick compares each ad set with its campaign's average and takes actions those rules cannot, such as duplicating and resetting.

    • 25,000+ campaigns optimised
    • 3M+ lines of code
    • Equivalent to 1,400+ automated rules
